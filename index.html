Aaj Prajakta se mujhe pata chala ki meri kuch baaton se tujhe laga ki main tujhe pasand karta hoon.

Main tujhe sirf apni best friend maanta tha. Teri care karta tha aur teri fikar thi, lekin mere dil mein tere liye kabhi koi romantic feelings nahi thi.

Agar meri kisi baat se tujhe bura laga ho ya uncomfortable feel hua ho, toh genuinely sorry. Mujhe maaf kar dena.

Tune dosti khatam karne ka decision liya hai, main uski respect karta hoon. Bas apni taraf se misunderstanding clear karni thi.

Apna khayal rakhna.
